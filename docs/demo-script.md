# Demo Script

1. Open Seoul Policy Data Map.
2. Confirm the header status: live city data or fallback rehearsal, update time, and active scenario.
3. Select `강남역 심야 혼잡 완화 확대` in Policy Composer.
4. Click `시뮬레이션 실행`.
5. Confirm the Run ID, execution timestamp, and verdict grade in Policy Composer.
6. Review Situation Signals and explain the four headline metrics.
7. Follow the Impact Graph from policy execution to operating pressure, daily-life impact, support, opposition, and verdict.
8. Use Reaction River to compare support, concern, opposition, and neutral reactions before and after launch.
9. Review Persona Cluster and identify which stakeholder groups need targeted communication.
10. Read Policy Verdict and mitigation notes.
11. Switch to `청계천 주말 접근 운영 변경`.
12. Run the simulation again and show how district, personas, and verdict change without changing the dashboard workflow.
