# Demo Script

1. Open Seoul Policy Data Map.
2. Confirm the header status: live rehearsal, freshness, and active scenario.
3. Select `강남역 심야 혼잡 완화 확대` in Policy Composer.
4. Review Situation Signals and explain the four headline metrics.
5. Follow the Impact Graph from policy execution to operating pressure, daily-life impact, support, opposition, and verdict.
6. Use Reaction River to compare support, concern, opposition, and neutral reactions before and after launch.
7. Review Persona Cluster and identify which stakeholder groups need targeted communication.
8. Read Policy Verdict and mitigation notes.
9. Switch to `청계천 주말 접근 운영 변경`.
10. Show how district, personas, and verdict change without changing the dashboard workflow.
